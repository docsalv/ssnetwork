QUICK CUSTOMIZATION:
1. Webinar registration:
   In index.html, replace https://forms.google.com/ with your actual Google Form/registration URL.
2. Instagram:
   Replace every https://www.instagram.com/ with the official SS Network Instagram URL.
3. Speakers:
   Replace Speaker One, Speaker Two and Speaker Three with the confirmed names, roles and bios.
4. Webinar date/time:
   In scripts.js, edit:
   const webinarDate = new Date("2026-10-24T18:00:00+01:00").getTime();
5. Partnership email:
   Replace hello@salvationsarkinetwork.com with the real partnership email.
